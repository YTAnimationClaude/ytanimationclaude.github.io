Privacy policy — shorts-publisher

Last updated: November 19, 2026

shorts-publisher is an internal tool used only by its owners to publish their own videos to their own social media accounts. It has no other users.

What data it accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos, and read-only access to the connected channel's name, id and list of uploads, used to confirm it is posting to the right channel and to avoid posting the same video twice.
  • Instagram (Meta): the connected account's id and username, permission to publish Reels, and the captions of its recent posts, used for the same two checks.
  • TikTok: the connected account's id and display name, and permission to send a video to the account's drafts.

How the data is used, stored and shared

  • The data is used only to publish the owners' videos to the owners' accounts.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted as GitHub Actions secrets in a private repository. No other personal data is stored, apart from the ids and links of the videos it published.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • shorts-publisher's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, for Instagram in the account's settings under apps and websites, and for TikTok under the account's security settings. Deleting the repository's secrets deletes the stored tokens.
