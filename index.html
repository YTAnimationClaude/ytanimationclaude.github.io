shorts-publisher

shorts-publisher is a private, internal tool. It publishes short animated educational videos that we make ourselves to our own channels on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, at scheduled times.

It is not offered to the public. It only signs in to accounts that we own, with our explicit permission, and it does nothing other than upload our own videos and check which account it is connected to.
